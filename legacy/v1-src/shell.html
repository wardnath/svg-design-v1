How the full method works

1 · rasterize

Both sides become pixels

The design SVG is drawn to a canvas as an image. The build HTML goes through an SVG foreignObject, so the browser lays it out and paints it, and the algorithms only ever receive the RGBA buffer. A PNG screenshot from Playwright enters the same way.

2 · segment

Pixels become a containment tree

Edges are color steps above a threshold, from 1- and 2-pixel stencils so anti-aliased corners still close. Edge components and the regions between them form an alternating graph; a breadth-first walk from the frame border yields nesting. Closed components with a large hole are frames; the rest is ink, grouped into lines by gap/height ratio and into paragraphs by line spacing.

3 · align

Viterbi per level, inside matched parents

Children are put in XY-cut reading order and aligned by a first-order Viterbi pass. Emission compares kind, size and color, with text measured against the page’s estimated font scale. Transitions are free when displacement is consistent or a gap to the previous match is preserved, and a skipped element may close its gap. Skips, 2:1 text merges and an order-free rescue pass cover missing, extra and swapped elements.

4 · attribute

Each displacement is charged once

An element can be explained by its parent’s edges or center, the sibling it flows after or before, a sibling it was aligned with in the design, or a gap a missing sibling left. Each reference is an edge weighted by the residual it leaves; Chu–Liu/Edmonds picks the minimum arborescence rooted at the parent, so a cascade lands on its first element and two siblings can’t excuse each other. Rows and columns form flow chains anchored at the end that moved least, and a changed gap inside a chain can’t be explained away from the far side. Heights may follow content; text is compared by ink mass, so reflow is free.
